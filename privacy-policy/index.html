Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Playground may process in its static prototype state and what changes when payment or Google sign-in are enabled.

Data collected now

In the current static implementation, Playground stores interface settings such as language, theme, ticket state, and prototype account data inside your browser localStorage. The contact form opens your local email client and does not send data through a Playground server.

Data collected when external services are enabled

If Google sign-in or Stripe checkout is configured, those services may process account identifiers, payment details, and diagnostic data under their own privacy terms. Playground will only use the minimum data needed to authenticate users, grant ticket access, and respond to contact requests.
