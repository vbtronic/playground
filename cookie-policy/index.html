Cookie Policy

This policy covers local storage, browser state, and third-party cookies that may appear once payment or Google sign-in is enabled.

Essential storage

Playground currently uses browser storage for theme, language, prototype account state, and ticket preview state. These values are used to keep the interface consistent between visits.

Third-party cookies

If Google sign-in or Stripe checkout is activated, Google and Stripe may set cookies or similar browser storage to support authentication, fraud prevention, and payment processing. Those services control their own cookie behavior.

Managing storage

You can clear Playground local storage at any time from your browser settings. Doing so will reset saved preferences and prototype account state.
